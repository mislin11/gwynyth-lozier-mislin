gwynythloziermislin

Gwynyth Lozier Mislin was born in the United States, lived many years in Ohio and Switzerland, where she taught conversational English, translated tourist brochures, and designed theatrical costumes. She has an MFA in fiction, has studied nonfiction and lives in Chevy Chase, Maryland.
